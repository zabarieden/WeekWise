Privacy Policy - NOT10.ai

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This document describes how the NOT10.ai app ("the App", "the Service") collects, uses, stores, and shares information about its users ("you"). NOT10.ai is operated by Eden Zabari ("we", "the owner"). Using the App means you agree to this policy.

1. What information we collect

1.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account details: email address and password (the password is encrypted and never accessible to us - it's managed by our infrastructure provider, see section 3).
  • Nutrition data: meal descriptions, calorie counts, protein, water tracking, weight tracking.
  • New Me program: starting and target weight, the chosen plan (1,300/1,500), menu choices, meal and drink check-offs, and the time the medical disclaimer was accepted.
  • Consent record: the time you accepted the Terms of Use and this policy, the Terms version, and your confirmation of being 16 or older.
  • Sport data: activity type, duration, distance, personal motivation, notes, and workout photos (optional, only if you choose to upload them).
  • Schedule and tasks: recurring weekly tasks, events and notes for specific dates.
  • Personal financial data: income and expenses, amounts, categories, free-text notes.
  • Photos you upload voluntarily: written-recipe photos and workout photos - only when you choose to upload them.
  • Free text you type for AI-based features - see section 3.
  • Google Calendar (optional): only if you connect it - see section 3A.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Local preferences saved on your own device only (language, color theme, accessibility settings) - this information is not sent to our servers and stays on the device itself.
  • A Push notification token, if you enabled notifications - used only to send reminders you requested.

We do not use advertising tracking tools, third-party "cookies" for advertising purposes, or external analytics networks.

2. Why we collect this information

  • To provide the Service itself - saving and displaying the data you entered across devices/logins.
  • To enable AI-based features (Premium) - calorie estimation, recipe text extraction from photos, free-text schedule parsing.
  • To send reminders/notifications you requested.
  • To keep your schedule in sync with Google Calendar, if you connected it (section 3A).
  • Technical support and responding to inquiries.

3A. Google Calendar data (optional)

Connecting Google Calendar is optional and happens only if you choose it in the App's settings. You then sign in with Google and approve access to your calendars (Google permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar). The App uses this access only to show your schedule and keep it in sync - nothing else.

  • What the App reads: the title, date and time of the events in your Google calendars, plus technical identifiers (event and calendar IDs, version stamps) that keep both sides matched. These events are shown in the App's schedule and monthly calendar.
  • What the App writes: events, tasks and notes you add to dates in the App are created, updated and deleted in your Google Calendar, so both stay the same. Events that have a reminder in the App are saved in Google without a Google reminder, so you are not notified twice.
  • Change notifications: the App registers a notification channel with Google, so changes you make in Google Calendar appear in the App automatically.
  • Storage: the synced events and the access tokens Google issues are stored in our database (Supabase), on encrypted servers and separated by account, so other users cannot access them. The tokens are used only by the App's server functions that perform the sync.
  • Sharing: we do not sell Google Calendar data, do not use it for advertising and do not transfer it to anyone, with one exception: when you use the AI schedule assistant (Premium), the titles, dates and times of your upcoming schedule items - which may include events synced from Google Calendar - are sent to Anthropic together with your request, solely to carry it out (for example, to understand "move my dentist appointment"). Anthropic processes this data under its commercial terms and does not use it to train its models. Google Calendar data is never used to develop, improve or train AI models.
  • Human access: no person reads your Google Calendar data, unless you ask us to (for example, for support), it is needed for security or abuse investigations, or the law requires it.
  • Disconnecting and deletion: you can disconnect Google Calendar at any time in the App's settings - the App revokes its access with Google, stops the notification channel and deletes the stored tokens. Events already shown in the App stay there until you delete them. You can also remove the App's access at any time from your Google Account (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Deleting your account deletes all synced events and the connection; it does not delete anything from your Google Calendar itself.

Limited Use: NOT10.ai's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data security

Data is stored on encrypted servers, and account login is protected via a secure authentication mechanism (Supabase Auth). However, as with any online service, 100% absolute security cannot be guaranteed. We take reasonable, accepted measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Review the information saved about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information - most data can be edited directly within the App at any time.
  • Delete your account and all associated information - account deletion is available directly from within the App (in account settings). This deletion is final and irreversible, and deletes all data saved for you in the system, including the photos you uploaded.

For any request regarding your personal information that cannot be handled independently within the App, you can contact us using the details at the end of this document.

6. Data retention

Data is retained as long as the account is active. Upon account deletion, personal information is deleted from our production systems. Technical backup copies may be retained for a short additional period for disaster-recovery purposes only, and are deleted in the regular backup cycle.

7. Children

Use of the Service is permitted only from the age of 16 (see Section 5A of the Terms of Use), and the New Me program only from the age of 18. If we learn that information was collected from a child under 16, we will act to delete it.
