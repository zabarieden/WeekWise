Privacy Policy - Obeko

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This document describes how the Obeko app ("the App", "the Service") collects, uses, stores, and shares information about its users ("you"). Obeko is operated by Eden Zabari ("we", "the owner"). Using the App means you agree to this policy.

1. What information we collect

1.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account details: email address and password (the password is encrypted and never accessible to us - it's managed by our infrastructure provider, see section 3).
  • Nutrition data: meal descriptions, calorie counts, protein, water tracking, weight tracking.
  • Sport data: activity type, duration, distance, personal motivation, notes, and workout photos (optional, only if you choose to upload them).
  • Schedule and tasks: recurring weekly tasks, events and notes for specific dates.
  • Personal financial data: income and expenses, amounts, categories, free-text notes.
  • Photos you upload voluntarily: written-recipe photos and workout photos - only when you choose to upload them.
  • Free text you type for AI-based features - see section 3.2.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Local preferences saved on your own device only (language, color theme, accessibility settings) - this information is not sent to our servers and stays on the device itself.
  • A Push notification token, if you enabled notifications - used only to send reminders you requested.

We do not use advertising tracking tools, third-party "cookies" for advertising purposes, or external analytics networks.

2. Why we collect this information

  • To provide the Service itself - saving and displaying the data you entered across devices/logins.
  • To enable AI-based features (Premium) - calorie estimation, recipe text extraction from photos, free-text schedule parsing.
  • To send reminders/notifications you requested.
  • Technical support and responding to inquiries.

3. Sharing information with third parties

We do not sell your information to anyone. To operate the Service, certain information is processed by external infrastructure providers, as detailed below:

SupabaseDatabase, user authentication, file storage, the App's server functionsAll information detailed in section 1 - this is the Service's central storage provider
Anthropic (Claude AI)Powering AI features (Premium only)Only when you use a specific AI feature: a photo you uploaded (for written-recipe scanning) or the text you typed (for calorie estimation/schedule parsing). This information is sent solely for processing

Each of these providers is subject to its own privacy and security policy. We choose providers committed to accepted security standards.

4. Data security

Data is stored on encrypted servers, and account login is protected via a secure authentication mechanism (Supabase Auth). However, as with any online service, 100% absolute security cannot be guaranteed. We take reasonable, accepted measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Review the information saved about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information - most data can be edited directly within the App at any time.
  • Delete your account and all associated information - account deletion is available directly from within the App (in account settings). This deletion is final and irreversible, and deletes all data saved for you in the system.

For any request regarding your personal information that cannot be handled independently within the App, you can contact us using the details at the end of this document.

6. Data retention

Data is retained as long as the account is active. Upon account deletion, personal information is deleted from our production systems. Technical backup copies may be retained for a short additional period for disaster-recovery purposes only, and are deleted in the regular backup cycle.

7. Children

The Service is not specifically directed at children under 16. If we learn that information was collected from a child under this age without proper parental/guardian consent, we will act to delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in an update to the "Last updated" date at the top of the document. We recommend checking this document periodically.
