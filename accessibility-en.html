Accessibility Statement - NOT10.ai

Last updated: July 29, 2026

At NOT10.ai, we place great importance on providing equal and comfortable usability for all users, including people with disabilities. This statement is written with reference to accessibility regulations for people with disabilities common internationally, and follows the same principles as Israel's Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities regulations.

1. Accessibility accommodations currently in the app

As of the date of this update, the app includes the following accommodations, which can be enabled independently from the Settings screen:

  • High Contrast mode - to make text and elements stand out against a dark background.
  • Color filter - for users with color-perception difficulties.
  • Adjustable interface/text size - three size levels (small / medium / large), selectable at any time.
  • The interface supports both right-to-left (RTL) and left-to-right (LTR) text direction, according to the selected display language.

2. Known limitations

For full transparency: the app has not yet undergone a formal accessibility audit by a certified accessibility auditor, and full compatibility with screen readers (such as VoiceOver on Apple devices and TalkBack on Android) has not been systematically tested and verified. Some components of the app may not be optimally accessible for use with a screen reader. We consider this an ongoing improvement process and are working to improve accessibility gradually.

3. Reporting an accessibility issue

If you encounter a difficulty or accessibility barrier while using the app, we'd be glad to hear about it and address it. You can contact our accessibility coordinator directly:

4. Contacting additional bodies

If you did not receive an adequate response from our accessibility coordinator, you may contact your country's relevant disability-rights authority (for example, in Israel: the Commission for Equal Rights of Persons with Disabilities at the Ministry of Justice).

5. Updating this statement

This accessibility statement will be updated from time to time as additional accommodations are added to the app, and as a formal accessibility audit is carried out.
